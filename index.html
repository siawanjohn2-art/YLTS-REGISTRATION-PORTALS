Event overview

Raising leaders because of souls

YLTS’26 trains youth leaders from Section 5 to serve with skill and heart. Expect teaching sessions, workshops, prayer, and time with leaders from across the fellowship.

Who should join

Youth leaders, officers, and members of Section 5 churches.

What you’ll do

Attend sessions, join workshops, and serve alongside your local youth team.

    Where is YLTS’26?

    Beatitudes of Jesus Christ Academy of Davao Inc., Deca Homes, Purok 8, Tacunan, Davao City. The dates are December 28 to 30, 2026.

    How much does it cost?

    Registration is ₱450. The event shirt is optional and costs ₱280, paid separately.

    Is there a sibling discount?

    Yes. For 3 siblings, two pay the full ₱450 and one pays half (₱225). For 4 siblings, three pay the full ₱450 and one is free. Register each sibling with the same family code.

    How do I register?

    Create an account on the Register page. Your dashboard shows your registration status and ticket.

    Can I edit my details later?

    Yes. Log in, open your dashboard, and choose Edit details.

    How do I pay the registration fee?

    After you submit your registration, scan the GCash QR on your dashboard, pay the fee, then enter your GCash reference number. Your ticket is released once the payment is submitted.

    What should I bring?

    A Bible, a notebook, your ticket code, and a valid ID.
